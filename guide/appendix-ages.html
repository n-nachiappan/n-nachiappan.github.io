Key Ages & Deadlines

The idea

  • Retirement runs on a schedule of ages — each one opens a door: a bigger contribution, a penalty-free withdrawal, a benefit you can finally claim, or a distribution you're forced to take.
  • Missing one is expensive: enroll in Medicare late and you pay a lifelong penalty; roll a 401(k) to an IRA before 59½ and you can forfeit the Rule of 55; skip an RMD and it's a 25% hit.
  • Ages are the same every year; the dollar figures attached to them change — see Appendix B for the 2026 numbers.

Read this as a timeline. The chapter links point to the full explanation of each rule.

What locks your Social Security benefit — and when

Your PIA is built from three inputs, each of which freezes at a fixed point and never changes afterward:

Highest 35 years of earnings — fewer than 35 working years means $0 fills the gaps and drags the average down
each indexed to the AWI of the year you turn 60 — that one year's wage index restates all earlier earnings in today's dollars
then run through the bend points fixed at age 62 — the dollar thresholds are set once and don't move

After age 62, only the annual COLA adjusts your benefit. The 35-year average and the age-60 AWI feed your AIME; the age-62 bend points convert that AIME into your PIA. Full walkthrough in Chapter 5.

The Roth 5-year clocks (duration, not age)

Two Roth rules are measured in years held, not by your age — and they're easy to trip over:

RuleWhat it meansCh.
Roth IRA earnings clockTo withdraw earnings tax-free you must be 59½ and have held any Roth IRA for 5 years — the clock starts with your first-ever Roth IRA contribution. (Your contributions can always come out tax- and penalty-free.)3
Roth conversion clockEach conversion has its own 5-year clock before the converted amount can be withdrawn penalty-free if you're under 59½ — convert early and often to keep the clocks running.12

Ages and rules reflect the 2026 plan year. Dollar figures change annually (Appendix B); reconfirm current-year numbers before acting. Nothing here is personalized advice.
