Key Numbers (2026)

The idea

  • Every dollar figure and threshold the guide leans on, gathered in one place for the 2026 plan year.
  • Almost all of these are indexed to inflation and reset each year — the mechanics stay the same, the numbers drift up. A few (the Social Security benefit-taxation thresholds and the top IRMAA tier) are fixed in law and don't move.
  • Pair this with Appendix A for the ages these figures attach to.
