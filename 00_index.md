# Retirement

### A Field Guide to Building, Spending, and Passing On Your Savings

*By Nachiappan, in collaboration with Claude.*
*A standalone field guide, paired with a free calculator to model your own numbers.*

---

Most retirement books are about *getting to* retirement — how to save, how much to save, how to invest along the way. This one is about the harder, stranger problem on the other side: once the paychecks stop and the balance is what it is, **how do you turn a pile of savings into a paycheck that lasts the rest of your life?**

That's the *decumulation* problem, and it's where the real money is made and lost. It runs on a tangle of interacting rules — the 4% rule, Social Security timing, Medicare surcharges, required distributions, Roth conversions, the tax code — that no single book usually covers in one place. This guide does. Each chapter pairs with the calculator so you can stop reading about a decision and actually *model* it against your own numbers.

Every chapter uses the same structure: a **Summary** of the key points, the **Details** with at least one worked example, a short list of **Action items**, and a **Model it** callout that shows how to test the idea in the tool.

### How the four "differentiator" chapters fit

Chapters 2, 3, 5, and 8 — inflation, healthcare, IRMAA, and sequencing — are the white-space topics most retirement books scatter or skip. If you read nothing else, read those four; they're where the biggest avoidable mistakes hide.
